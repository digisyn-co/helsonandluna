# Prompt for Gemini — the window-seat view (chapter 07, "The crossing")

Paste everything below the line. Attach nothing, or attach one of the couple's photos only if you
want the light and grade matched to them.

---

Create a **photorealistic photograph** of the view out of an aircraft window in flight, as if
shot on a phone from a window seat at cruising altitude.

This image sits *behind* an existing cabin-window frame in a web design, so it must be the
**view only**:

- **No window frame, no window seal, no cabin wall, no cabin interior, no seat, no armrest,
  no reflections of an interior.** Just the world outside.
- No text, no watermark, no logo, no border, no vignette.

## The shot

- A wide sea of soft cumulus cloud tops, seen from above, receding to a slightly curved horizon.
- The **aircraft wing entering from the lower left**, seen in sharp perspective foreground —
  real aluminium panelling, visible panel seams and rivets, flap hinges, a winglet turning up at
  the far end, faint heat haze and a hint of exhaust shimmer near the root.
- Low sun off the upper right: warm light raking across the wing's upper surface, a soft flare,
  clouds bright on their tops with cool grey-lavender shadowed undersides.
- A thin band of paler haze where sky meets cloud, and deeper blue at the very top of frame.

## Look

- Photographic realism: real lens characteristics, slight softness in the far distance, natural
  sensor noise, no illustration or CGI look, no over-sharpening, no HDR crunch.
- **Grade: pale warm blush.** Very low colour saturation, a soft rose-beige cast over everything,
  lifted blacks, ivory highlights, low contrast, fine film grain, gentle bloom in the brightest
  areas. Faded film photograph on warm ivory paper — not a vivid travel photo, not high-contrast
  black and white. Blues should be muted lavender-grey rather than saturated blue.

## Deliver two crops of the same photograph

1. `flight-portrait.jpg` — 1400 × 1750 px (4:5), the primary; the design is mobile-first.
2. `flight-wide.jpg` — 2400 × 1350 px (16:9), the same frame extended for desktop.

If you can only produce one, produce the portrait.

## Optional second image

`flight-clouds.jpg`, same specs and same grade, with **no wing at all** — pure cloud tops and
horizon, as a calmer alternative.
